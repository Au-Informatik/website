FAQ

How do I throw?

Put your thumb on the lower part of the screen, drag the crosshair onto the target and let go. Choose a sack at the bottom: hard, medium or soft – the flag on each target shows what it needs.

Why was I caught?

Throwing while the teacher is facing the class gets you caught. Watch out for the “Hm?!” – better wait a moment.

There is no sound.

Check the speaker button at the top right of the game and the iPhone’s silent switch.

My progress is gone.

Progress is stored only on the iPhone. Deleting the app removes it.
