In short

The app collects no personal data, has no user account, shows no ads and does not follow you across other apps or websites (no tracking). To help us improve the game, from version 1.5 it reports anonymously how it is played (see “Anonymous statistics”) – you can switch this off in the settings. If the app crashes, a technical error report is sent to us (see “Error reports”). Neither contains any information about you.

What is stored on your device

Game progress, your personal high scores and settings (sound, language, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s leaderboards through your Apple account, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. We receive no personal data beyond the leaderboards as publicly shown in Game Center. You can turn Game Center off in the iPhone settings.

Updates

On launch the app asks Expo’s update service (u.expo.dev) whether a new game version is available. Technical details (platform, app version) and – as with any internet request – your device’s IP address are transmitted. This data is used only to deliver updates.

Error reports (Sentry)

So that we can fix crashes and errors, the app sends a technical error report to the service Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) when this happens. The data is stored in the EU (data centre in Frankfurt). The report contains details such as device model, iOS and app version, the place in the program where the error occurred and how long individual steps took. It contains no name, no account and no content from your device; the IP address is not stored. The reports are used only to fix errors and are deleted after 90 days.

Anonymous statistics (PostHog)

From version 1.5 the app reports anonymously how the game is used: when the app is opened, which room is played and how a round ends (points, grade, hits, how often you were caught, duration). In addition: device model, iOS and app version, language and country. For this the app creates a random identifier that is stored only on your device and is not linked to any name, account or other apps. The recipient is the service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.); data is stored in the EU (data centre in Frankfurt). The IP address is used only to determine the country and is then discarded. There are no screen recordings and nothing is passed on to third parties for advertising. We use the figures only to see which rooms are too hard and whether new features work. You can switch the statistics off at any time in the app settings under “Anonymous statistics”.

This website

The website au-informatik.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, including your IP address, to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. The website sets no cookies and uses no tracking.

Ratings and speech

Rating requests are shown by the iPhone itself (Apple); we do not learn whether or how you rate. Spoken lines in the game are generated by the iPhone’s speech output; nothing is recorded or transmitted.

Children

The app is rated 13+ and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Since we store no personal data about you, there is nothing to disclose or delete. Questions: info@au-informatik.com.

Changes

If new features affect data (for example purchases or ads), we will update this policy beforehand.
