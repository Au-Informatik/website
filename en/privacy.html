In short

The app collects no personal data, has no user account, shows no ads and does not track you (no tracking, no analytics tools).

What is stored on your device

Game progress, your personal high scores and settings (sound, language, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s leaderboards through your Apple account, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. We receive no personal data beyond the leaderboards as publicly shown in Game Center. You can turn Game Center off in the iPhone settings.

Updates

On launch the app asks Expo’s update service (u.expo.dev) whether a new game version is available. Technical details (platform, app version) and – as with any internet request – your device’s IP address are transmitted. This data is used only to deliver updates.

Ratings and speech

Rating requests are shown by the iPhone itself (Apple); we do not learn whether or how you rate. Spoken lines in the game are generated by the iPhone’s speech output; nothing is recorded or transmitted.

Children

The app is rated 13+ and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Since we store no personal data about you, there is nothing to disclose or delete. Questions: admin@banifrutta.com.

Changes

If new features affect data (for example purchases or ads), we will update this policy beforehand.
